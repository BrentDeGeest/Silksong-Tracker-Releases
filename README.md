# Silksong Map Tracker

**A free, unofficial companion map for Hollow Knight: Silksong.** Every item, boss, bench and shortcut on one map, with directions, a story guide, builds and a helper you can just ask.

### ▶ [Open the app in your browser](https://brentdegeest.github.io/Silksong-Tracker-Releases/)

Works on phones, tablets and computers, also on iPhone. Tap **Add to home screen** in your browser's menu and it opens like a normal app, even without internet.

Android? You can also install the app itself: download `app-debug.apk` from the [latest release](https://github.com/BrentDeGeest/Silksong-Tracker-Releases/releases/latest).

![The main menu](https://github.com/BrentDeGeest/Silksong-Tracker-Releases/releases/download/V36/1-menu.jpg)

## What it does

- **The whole map**: Mask Shards, Spool Fragments, fleas, bosses, benches, Bellways, shops, levers, breakable walls and doors, each with a picture and a plain explanation of when, how and why.
- **Directions**: tap a pin and get a route from where you are, with Bellway rides and shortcuts.
- **Made for new players**: a story guide step by step, "Get stronger" tips, ready-made builds, and a list of things you can miss.
- **The Helper**: ask "where is Moss Mother?", "take me to the nearest bench" or "bigger icons", and it does it.
- **Your progress is saved** on your device, with a backup code to move it.
- **Four styles**: Silksong, Hollow Knight, Greenpath and Classic, plus colour-blind modes.

Free for everyone. No account, no ads, nothing to buy.

## Found a mistake or have an idea?

Use the ⚠ button on any card in the app. Every report is read.
